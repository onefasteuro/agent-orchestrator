# Brief shape

Prose, under about 80 lines, appended after the role prompt in `worker-start --spec`. The worker is a fresh session: everything it needs is here or in its checkout.

```
=== TASK task-<n>: <one-line outcome> ===

GOAL
<What must be true when you are done, in two or three sentences.>

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (from specs/<feature>/spec.md at commit <sha>)
<Paste the criteria this task must satisfy, verbatim.>

RULES
<Paste the shared rules from the spec and the relevant lines of AGENTS.md.>

WORKSPACE
Checkout: <absolute path>    Base commit: <BASE>
You may edit: <paths>
You may create: <paths>
Generated by hooks, do not touch: <paths>
Everything else is off limits, including tests you were not assigned, specs/, AGENTS.md, .agents/, .claude/, .githooks/ and .git/.

CHECKS (run exactly as written, report the result line)
- <name>: `<command>`, expect `<line>`
- full suite: `npm test`, expect `<pass n fail m>`

LIMITS
At most <k> files and <m> changed lines. One sitting. Ask the coordinator with the preamble's ask command when the brief does not settle a decision or after two failed attempts at the same problem; then stop editing until the answer arrives.

REPORT
One worker_done: changed paths as git status shows them, each check with its result line, questions asked and answers received, remaining uncertainty.
```

For a reviewer: replace WORKSPACE with the review checkout, `BASE` and the candidate SHA, add "Diff: `git diff <BASE>..<CAND>`", state the pass number, and in pass 1 add nothing from the implementer. Ask for `VERDICT: ACCEPT` or `VERDICT: REPAIR` as the first line.

For an advisor or debugger: GOAL becomes the one question with the files involved, what was tried and what was observed; WORKSPACE says whether the tree is uncommitted.

For a researcher: GOAL is the one question and the permitted sources; add `REPORT PATH: .agents/orca-sdd/runs/<feature>/<task>-report.md` and ask for a three-sentence `worker_done` body with `--report-path`.
