## Lessons recorded immediately

- The coordinator script must branch on the validator's exit status. The CLI already exits 1 on failure; the mistake was piping its output into a parser and losing the status. Workflow step 3.4 now needs the wording "stop on non-zero exit", and the entrypoint gets the same rule.
- Hash computation belongs in the tools, not in ad hoc shell. Add `sdd doc-identity --repo --revision --path` so the coordinator never hashes by hand.
- An invalid dispatch that already wrote files is handled by preserving its diff as an artifact and resetting the task worktree, with a `recovery_reconciled` event, before the valid attempt starts.
