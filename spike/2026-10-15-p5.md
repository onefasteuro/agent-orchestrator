## Calibration

Candidates on branches `calib/*`, each a single commit on the pilot base, reviewed with pass 1 only, ground truth withheld. Three blocks were run, one per reviewer prompt version, as K-19 requires after a prompt change:

- Block 1: original `roles/reviewer.md`. Seeds 5 of 5 caught. Controls: C1, C2 accepted; C3, C4 flagged MEDIUM for missing validation and no-mutation tests (implementation judged correct); C5 was a coordinator briefing error (reviewed against AC-1 while implementing `count`). A user-requested recheck of C3 and C4 on fresh sessions found the same coverage gap each time, rated LOW once (ACCEPT) and medium three times (REPAIR): detection stable, severity unstable.
- User adjudication (D-21): the coverage finding is legitimate; add a MEDIUM severity floor for untested spec-required behaviour and rerun.
- Block 2: floor worded as "a behaviour the specification requires with no test". Seeds 5 of 5. Controls: every control with any missing sub-case flagged, including C1, C2, C3b, C4b; only C5b accepted. The wording made the reviewer demand every sub-case (string element, plus and minus Infinity), an infinite regress.
- Block 3: floor refined to rule level (a required rule with no test is MEDIUM; a tested rule missing sub-cases is LOW). Seeds 5 of 5. Controls C1b, C2b, C3c, C4c, C5c (each with a test per required rule and a no-mutation test): 5 of 5 accepted.
